Axelera AI's Pioneer 10 Project Challenge asked developers to build computer-vision systems on Axelera's Metis edge-AI hardware that trigger real-world action. Umar's DrainSentinel, which spots blocked drains and alerts sanitation authorities before they cause flooding, was one of 10 projects selected and received a hardware kit for the build. It was the only selected project from Africa.
