UNICEF's Office of Innovation ran the Climate Innovation Challenge with the India Health Fund and the Cambridge Institute for Sustainability Leadership. From more than 1,200 startups across emerging markets, five ventures were selected for mentorship and investment-readiness support through the UNICEF Venture Fund. ClimatrixAI was the only African venture chosen. UNICEF describes it as combining satellite imagery, IoT sensors and AI for hyperlocal predictions of floods, heatwaves and other hazards.
