Qualcomm Make in Africa is an equity-free mentorship programme for African deep-tech startups working on edge AI, IoT and 5G. ClimatrixAI was one of 10 startups selected for the 2025 cohort from about 435 applications across 19 countries, for AI-driven hyperlocal flood risk prediction and monitoring.
