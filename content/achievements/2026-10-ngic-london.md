The NEXTGEN Innovation Challenge is run by Nigeria's National Board for Technology Incubation (NBTI) with UK partner UKALD. After a boot camp in Abuja, finalists pitch to global investors at a grand finale in London. Umar received an award at the October 2026 finale, where 298 Nigerian innovators were showcased.
